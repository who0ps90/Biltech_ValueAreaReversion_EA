# Assumptions and unresolved limitations

- This release is restricted to the MT5 Strategy Tester. It cannot place live or demo-account trades. No account was connected and no orders were placed while producing these files.
- Profiles use completed M1 OHLCV bars. Uniform allocation over each bar's price range is an approximation to volume at price; the actual path and within-bar activity are unknown. Tick volume measures broker quote activity, not centralized exchange volume.
- Each profile uses exactly the immediately preceding analytical session. It is frozen for the following session. Invalid or substantially incomplete sessions are skipped; there is no rolling search for an older valid profile.
- AUTO chooses a single usable source from the profile and pins it for that profile's entire trading session. If required real volume subsequently becomes unavailable on a signal candle, the profile becomes unusable for the rest of that session. It cannot switch to tick volume mid-session. Strict source consistency may skip data for which some other definition would trade.
- Deterministic histogram and signal tie/boundary behavior is documented in the README. Tick alignment can reduce effective row count. A degenerate one-price profile is represented by one tick-wide bin; ordinary tick-aligned closes have no strictly interior tick price, so reclaim cannot qualify.
- New York calendar handling implements current post-2007 US DST rules for 2007–2099. Broker mapping must be explicitly fixed or follow the supported EU/US convention for the entire run. It is not an IANA historical timezone database. Unusual broker transitions or policy changes require splitting history or extending the clock with reviewed rules. Missing/ambiguous mapping fails initialization or locks runtime operation until corrected and restarted.
- Default data coverage requires at least 90% of elapsed UTC session minutes as completed M1 bars as well as the absolute minimum and gap checks. Some genuine shortened/holiday sessions and broker maintenance gaps are rejected. Coverage tests cannot establish that bars are authentic or that all broker ticks exist. M1 coverage does not certify real-tick coverage.
- Entry windows and forced-exit time use New York local time even in custom broker-session mode. Broker custom session boundaries affect the profile; they do not redefine the New York risk day.
- Signals use completed candles and an explicit next-bar entry deadline. A delayed/stale event is rejected; a failed first-tick entry is not retried. This can differ from systems that keep an order intention alive.
- Protective stops and targets can fill beyond requested prices during gaps. No software loss threshold guarantees a maximum realized loss. Exit attempts may fail while a market is closed or constraints prevent execution; close requests remain due.
- Risk sizing relies on MT5's symbol/account profit and margin calculations. The estimate of round-trip commission and slippage is used for sizing and minimum R/R; actual costs come from Tester deal history. Verify both. Broker-specific commissions/fees posted outside attributable deals cannot be reconstructed reliably as this EA's cost.
- A `CLOSED` summary reflects costs available when that position is first observed flat. Any later adjustments still appear as individual `DEAL` records and must be reconciled with the Tester history before accepting aggregate results; the provided summary analyzer does not revise an earlier completed-position row.
- Initial fill monetary risk must be recorded at the fill time. A past fill without that record has unavailable R; later exchange rates are not used to fabricate original account-currency risk.
- Daily loss uses this EA's realized net and floating P/L against the recorded New York day-start equity. A first partial day needs an explicit baseline or stays locked. Existing EA activity with a missing initial day snapshot remains locked even with an explicit baseline. After a restart, the journal and history reconstruct state; lost journal state cannot prove a past equity baseline, so the EA fails closed rather than resetting the allowance.
- One instance per symbol and test is the supported baseline. A shared netting symbol cannot safely separate manual/other-magic exposure; ownership conflicts block operation. This is not a portfolio risk system. Independent tests require new run IDs, rather than reusing another experiment's journal.
- The tab-delimited private journal (with `.csv` extension), public quoted CSV logs and normal Tester deal history are part of the research evidence. Journaled audit rows recover interrupted writes, while malformed/partial CSVs are rejected. Continuation requires matching account/configuration identity and the continued presence of recorded entry deals. Deleted/modified files or incomplete history can prevent exact recovery. Never merge unrelated runs by editing identifiers. Files are local to the tester agent.
- No MetaEditor compilation, MQL self-test execution, broker-contract execution tests, visual audit, or historical backtest is claimed unless recorded in `Docs/Validation.md`. Source and host-tool checks do not prove MT5 runtime behavior.
